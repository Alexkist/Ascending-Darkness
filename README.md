Ascending Darkness turns your world into a Nightmare. Will you be able to fight against the horrors that now plague your world? Only time will tell...

# Focus
**The core focus of Ascending Darkness is to add horrifying entities to your world, as well as new horrifying structers to explore.** The Modpack also includes Boss Fights and new Equipment to help you fight against the horrors that now plague it, as well as Quality of Life improvements, Decorations and new ways to protect your Base.

# Recommendations
We recommend you use [Insanity Shaders](https://modrinth.com/shader/insanity-shader) with [Oculus](https://modrinth.com/mod/oculus) or [True Darkness Biomes](https://modrinth.com/mod/true-darkness-biomes) for a better experience. **The True Darkness fork linked here includes an option for certain Biomes where True Darkness does not apply.**

# Compatibility
[YUNG's API](https://modrinth.com/mod/yungs-api) is included within the Modpack by default. **You will however need [Cataclysm x YUNG's Better Nether Fortresses Compat](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/cataclysm-x-yungs) if you use [YUNG's Better Nether Fortresses](https://modrinth.com/mod/yungs-better-nether-fortresses) to allow Ignited Berserkers to spawn after defeating Ignis.**

# Multiplayer
The Modpack will also work in a Multiplayer environment. However, **Simple Voicechat's Group feature is disabled by default.** Please use [Simple Radio](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-radio) for communcation for the best experience. **It is recommended to keep Groups disabled, but you can enable Groups again if you wish.**

**The Server will also store conversations from Voicechat, which can then be used by entities to mimic a Player's voice.** For more information on this, check out the Modrinth Page of [Mimicked](https://modrinth.com/mod/mimicked).

# Apollyon
To get started with Apollyon, you need to find the "Council Room", this Structer generates in the Overworld and was built out of Deepslate and Polished Basalt, within the Building you can find "The Infector".

### The Infector
- Spawns in the "Council Room"
- The entity is a Villager, infected with something.
- He is friendly, at first...
- After he explaines his Story, he will succumb to his infection.
  * Right after he finishes, be ready to run.
  * The Infector will chase the Player for a short time, breaking every block in its path.

### The Damned
- "The Damned" is the main entity of Apollyon
- A creature composing of a long Neck, two floating hands and a wide smile...
- If you get too close, one of two things can happen,
  * It teleports away, giving you the Darkness effect
  * It starts hunting you
- The entity can be killed, but it will return...

### The Fallen
- A tall, slim creature
- Getting too close will
  * Trigger a Jumpscare
  * After it disapears, it drops a Wither Rose

# The Knocker
- The Knocker knows where you live
  * He may sometimes wait for you to return home
- The Knocker can randomly appear at Night or in the Day, preventing you from sleeping
- The Knocker can Jumpscare you

# The Anomaly
This Page will never go into detail on how the Entity works, the Mod is intended for long Playthroughs and to be played without much knowledge of the Entity.
- Starts spawning after about 7 days (default)
  * Can be skipped by using the Totem **(Use at your own risk...)**
- The Entity's behavior is unknown
  * The Owner does not respond to most questions related to The Anomaly's behavior.
- The Anomaly can be killed, but returns after about 7 days. **(Uncertain)**

# The End of Herobrine
- Herobrine starts in your world, he does not need to be summoned
- Herobrine can randomly spawn around the Player
  * If you get too close to Herobrine or look at him for too long, he will teleport and/or walk towards you, giving you Darkness and Blindness
- Herobrine can Build structures
  * Letters of his Name
  * Traps
  * Signs
- Herobrine can Possess Mobs
  * He cannot Possess Cows, Sheep, Villagers or Zombie Villagers
  * These can be changed in `endofherobrine-common.toml`

# Born in Chaos
- Please check out Born in Chaos's [Wiki Page](https://borninchaosmod.fandom.com/wiki/Born_in_Chaos_Minecraft_Mod_Wiki)
- You can edit Settings in `borninconfiguration-general.toml`
  * This is a feature of [Born In Configuration](https://modrinth.com/mod/born-in-configuration)

# The Graveyard
**This section only details how to summon the Corrupted Champion**, nothing more. You can also view a Guide on its [Modrinth Page](https://modrinth.com/mod/the-graveyard-forge)

### Fragments
Collect three bone staff pieces (Ominous Bone Staff Fragment) from Ruins, each Ruin has a unique fragment (Upper, Middle and Lower Bone Staff Fragment)

### Getting a Vail of Blood
Collect a Bone Dagger and craft a Glass Bottle, after this put the Glass Bottle in your offhand and start killing Villagers with the Bone Dagger, you will need to repeat this until the Vail is fully filled.

### Spawning the Champion
Find the Lich Prison structure, an Island floating above the Water.

![Guide to spawning Lich 1](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/733f0abfa2bb840f76cfcf3be4a8ed1ae6c06837.png)

Place all Ominous Bone Staff Fragments (Upper to Lower) infront of the Altar like this
**After you have placed all Fragment, wait for Night and Pour the Vail of Blood over the Altar**, have fun!

If a Player is killed by The Damned, the Server crashes a minute later due to the Server Watchdog.

**While not confirmed and quite Inconsistent**, it may just be Apollyon and another Mod interfearing with each other. The Owner of the Mod has already been informed.
